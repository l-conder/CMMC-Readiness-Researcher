    What this is. A folder-canonical demonstration of the CMMC Readiness Researcher, an ICM (Interpretable Context Methodology) build. The folder is the brain; this page is a viewer over it. Scenarios are scripted from the researcher's own examples.md and DEEP_TEST.md.

    What it is not. It does not certify anyone (only a C3PAO or the government does), does not give legal advice, and does not make the binding CUI determination — that comes from your contract and your government customer. For AI on real controlled data, the compliant route is a FedRAMP-authorized deployment, not a tool like this.
